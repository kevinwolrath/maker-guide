Synthetic Epoxy Resin Dice Guide

For a three colour swirl, divide prepared epoxy resin into three cups, add the chosen colours, then pour the colours into the dice mould in alternating passes. Use a clean tool for a gentle swirl without fully blending the colours.

To reduce bubbles in resin dice, mix slowly and avoid whipping air into the resin. After pouring, use a pressure pot when the resin system and equipment instructions allow it; pressure helps compress visible bubbles while the resin cures.

When handling uncured epoxy resin, wear suitable protective gloves and work with adequate ventilation. Follow the resin manufacturer's Safety Data Sheet for product-specific PPE and handling requirements.
